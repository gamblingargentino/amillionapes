# Reference game analysis

Notes from screenshots of Survive a Million Monkeys. These describe what it does, so we can decide what to keep and what to change.

## Structure

- **Lobby.** Vehicle pads for squad matchmaking (each shows slots like 0/4), shop, invite friends, weapons, classes, titles, a shooting range, leaderboards (highest wave, most kills), a starter-pack offer with a countdown.
- **Lobby creation.** Pick map (Cove, Laboratory), lobby size 1 to 4, mode (normal), access (public or private).
- **In run.** Wave counter, next-boss bar with segments, skip-wave vote with auto-skip toggle, heal-all and nuke-all buttons, cash upgrades on the left (health, shotgun), three abilities on the right including a charged airstrike ultimate, map weapon pickups.
- **Results.** XP breakdown by waves, kills, new best and bosses, then gems earned, squad kills, wave reached and time survived with "new best" tags.

## Progression

- Gems (permanent) fund weapon tiers 1 to 4 with star upgrades and a class ladder from common to legendary.
- Titles reward milestones such as defeating 1,000,000 monkeys or reaching wave 100.
- One legendary class is also the main bundle for Robux.

## What it does well

Short clear loop, two economies, a boss goal per run, easy squad entry, long-term chase goals.

## Where we can differ

- Enemy variety beyond one family.
- Run-to-run build variety through perks.
- Map structure with cash-gated zones.
- A seasonal event system built in from day one.
