# Classes, weapons and perks

## Classes

Each class has two cash abilities and one ultimate that charges over the run. The reference game uses a ladder of seven. We keep the same archetypes under our own names and lore.

| Archetype | Job in the squad | Rarity tier |
|---|---|---|
| Rifleman (starter) | All-round, grenade and barricade | Common |
| Medic | Heals and revives | Uncommon |
| Engineer | Turrets and walls | Rare |
| Heavy | Soaks hits, draws aggro | Rare |
| Demolitions | Area damage | Epic |
| Flamethrower | Burns clusters, counters swarms | Epic |
| Marksman (legendary) | Boss damage, weak-point shots | Legendary |

Engineer is the anchor for the base-building twist (see [maps](04-maps.md)).

## Weapons

- Four tiers. Each tier has several weapons, and each weapon has star upgrades that raise damage per second and fire rate.
- Weapons can be bought mid-run with cash, and rare map pickups drop strong one-use weapons.
- A "try" button in the weapon screen lets players test any weapon on the lobby range before spending gems.

## Perk draft

Every few waves each player picks one of three perks. Perks last for the run only. Starter list:

- Extra damage against machines, or against apes.
- Kill streak refills ultimate faster.
- Reloads heal a little.
- Ability cooldowns shorter.
- Cash from kills up by a percent.
- Teammate revive time shorter.
- Explosions chain to nearby enemies.
- Weak-point hits deal bonus damage.

Perks stack with cash upgrades, which is where run-to-run variety comes from.
