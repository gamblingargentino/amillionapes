# Vision and pillars

Working title: **Iron Apes** (not final, see [decisions](12-decisions-and-open-questions.md)).

## Pitch

A Roblox co-op (1-4 players) round-based horde shooter. A squad holds out in an abandoned military research base while waves of apes, leftover war machines and ape-machine hybrids get stronger every round. Between runs, players spend gems on weapons and classes. A Halloween event version runs in a foggy cornfield map.

## Pillars

1. **Readable escalation.** Each wave range introduces one new idea, and players can tell a new threat from its silhouette.
2. **Every run different.** Cash upgrades plus a perk draft mean two runs rarely play the same.
3. **Co-op roles.** Classes have a clear job in the squad.
4. **Seconds to start.** Pad matchmaking, a shooting range in the lobby, no tutorial wall.
5. **Live events.** Seasonal skins and bosses ride on the same systems (see [Halloween event](05-halloween-event.md)).

## What we change from the reference game

| Reference | Our version |
|---|---|
| One enemy family (monkeys) | Apes, machines, then hybrids that mix both rule sets ([enemies](02-enemies.md)) |
| Flat cash upgrades | Cash upgrades plus a pick-1-of-3 perk draft ([core loop](01-core-loop.md)) |
| Single open arena | Zones unlocked with cash, map hazards, boss arenas ([maps](04-maps.md)) |

Full breakdown of the reference: [reference game analysis](research/reference-game-analysis.md).

## Non-goals for v1

- No PvP, no trading, no open world.
- No more than two maps at launch.
- No custom matchmaking beyond lobby size and public/private.
