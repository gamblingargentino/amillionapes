# Monetization and live ops

Keep paid items to convenience and cosmetics. Players should be able to reach every class by playing.

## Offers

- Gem packs.
- Starter pack with a time-limited discount.
- Class bundles (class, weapon, gems), for example a legendary class bundle.
- Gamepasses: double gems, extra loadout slot.
- Private lobbies free for everyone, because they help retention.

## Live ops

- Seasonal events built on the event config ([Halloween event](05-halloween-event.md)).
- Codes for launch and milestones.
- Weekly boss or mutator rotation to give players a reason to return.

## Rules

- Never sell damage or health that only paying players can have.
- Show prices and what is included clearly.
